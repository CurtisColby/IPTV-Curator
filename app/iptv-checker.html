After you press Save & Rebuild

1. In NostalgiaTV, refresh this source (or restart the app) so it re-reads the playlist and guide.

2. For custom channels you set up by hand: use the 📡 button next to the channel in My List — it copies the XMLTV URL and the exact Guide Channel id to enter in the channel editor. Only needed when a channel's guide choice changed.

3. Nothing to do day-to-day: guides refresh automatically every few hours and the TV picks them up on its own refresh.
